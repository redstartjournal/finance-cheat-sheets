# Contributing

Thank you for helping. These sheets are read by people who are new to money, so every word and number has to be right.

## Ways to help

1. **Report a mistake.** Open a "Correction" issue: which sheet, what is wrong, and a source that shows the right fact
   (a regulator, a law, an official statistic, the company's own report). We check it, fix the sheet and thank you in the
   change.
2. **Suggest a sheet.** Open a "Suggest a sheet" issue: the idea, who it helps, and the sources you would use.
3. **Improve the text.** Pull requests that fix typos or make the text under a sheet clearer are welcome.

## What we can't take

- Redrawn or edited versions of the sheets (the licence is no-derivatives, and the drawings are made in one style).
- Advice on what to buy, sell or avoid, or rankings of brokers, funds or shares.
- Facts without a source.

## The small print

By opening an issue or a pull request you agree that Red Start Journal may use your suggestion in its sheets and lessons
under the same licence. We credit every accepted correction in the commit that makes it.

Be kind. Discussions stay about the facts.
